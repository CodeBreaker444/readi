The AI Assistant lets you ask questions in plain language about missions, maintenance, compliance, and safety. It only shows information that your role and your organisation are allowed to see.

## How It Works

When you ask a question, the assistant:

1. **Understands what you need** — for example a question about your data, a company procedure, a general web lookup, or equipment that is no longer in service.
2. **Finds the right information** — it looks only in the areas your role has access to.
3. **Applies privacy rules** — it only uses your organisation's data. Pilots only see their own missions, and sensitive details such as passwords and purchase prices are never shown.
4. **Prepares a clear answer** — it combines your data with company procedures and, when useful, general web information.

To keep answers fast and readable, the assistant looks at a limited number of records at a time (up to 15). For large results, ask a more specific question, such as a narrower date range.

## Who Can Ask About What

| Role | What the assistant can help with |
|---|---|
| **Pilot in Command (PIC)** | Your own missions and results, planning, your declarations, notifications, and shared files |
| **Operations Manager (OPM)** | Everything a pilot can see, plus shifts, checklists, documents, users, equipment and maintenance, audits, compliance, safety reports, alerts, training, and clients |
| **Safety Manager (SM)** | Safety reports and actions, safety indicators, alerts, missions, equipment, maintenance, and training attendance |
| **Accountable Manager (AM)** | Missions, planning, equipment and its maintenance, documents, audits, and compliance |
| **Compliance Monitoring Manager (CMM)** | Audits and findings, compliance requirements, evidence and status history, and safety indicators |
| **Maintenance Manager (MM)** | Equipment, parts, maintenance work and its history, missions, and alerts |
| **Training Manager (TM)** | Training, attendance, safety indicators, and user profiles |
| **Data Controller (DC)** | Users and profiles, permissions, missions, and shared files |
| **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** | Missions, equipment, maintenance, safety indicators, alerts, and clients |
| **Administrator** | Same as Operations Manager, once acting as an Operations Manager |

## Current Limits

The assistant cannot yet answer some questions:

| Topic | What you can't ask yet |
|---|---|
| Evaluations | "Which evaluations are still open?" |
| Pilot qualifications | "Which pilots have expired qualifications?" |
| Pilot declarations (for managers) | "Did pilot X file a pre-flight declaration?" |
| Flight incidents | Incident flags, unplanned returns home, lost connection, and route deviations |
| Battery and fuel | Battery charge and fuel use during a flight |

For security, the assistant never reveals passwords, login details, access keys, or purchase prices.

---

## Built-in Safety Checks

When asked to review missions, the assistant automatically flags a **violation** in these cases:

1. **Wind** — the mission flew while a wind alert was still open.
2. **Altitude** — the drone flew higher than 120 metres.
3. **Maintenance** — the drone had an open high-priority maintenance issue at the time of the flight.

It can also list **equipment parts that have reached their expiry date** and are no longer in service, grouped by system.

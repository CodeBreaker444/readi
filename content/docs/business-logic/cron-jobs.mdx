# Cron Jobs

## Overview

Scheduled background jobs are defined in `vercel.json` and run as Vercel Cron Jobs. Each job is protected by a shared secret (`CRON_SECRET`), so only the scheduler can trigger it.

**Execution pattern:** all jobs fetch every active owner (`owner_active: 'Y'`) and loop over them, processing/sending notifications per owner independently. A failure for one owner is caught and logged; it does not stop processing of the remaining owners.

## Schedule Reference

| Job | Schedule (UTC) | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| `refresh-maintenance-days` | `0 */6 * * *` | Every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00) |
| `check-component-expiration` | `0 */6 * * *` | Every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00) |
| `check-maintenance-alerts` | `0 */6 * * *` | Every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00) |
| `check-insurance-expiration` | `0 */6 * * *` | Every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00) |
| `reset-email-counters` | `0 0 * * *` | Daily at 00:00 |
| `check-training-expiration` | `0 6 * * *` | Daily at 06:00 |

Schedules are defined in `vercel.json` at the project root and are evaluated in UTC by Vercel Cron.

## Job Details

### refresh-maintenance-days

- **Schedule:** `0 */6 * * *` (every 6 hours)
- **Purpose:** Recomputes "days remaining" style maintenance countdown fields for every active owner's systems/tools.
- **Sends email:** No — this is a data-refresh job, not a notification job.

### check-component-expiration

- **Schedule:** `0 */6 * * *` (every 6 hours)
- **Purpose:** Finds active tool components whose `expiration_date` has passed and sends expiration notifications, grouped by owner.
- **Sends email:** Yes, subject to the module/event enablement checks described in [Email Notification Business Logic](/business-logic/email-notifications).

### check-maintenance-alerts

- **Schedule:** `0 */6 * * *` (every 6 hours)
- **Purpose:** Evaluates maintenance alert triggers (e.g., usage/cycle thresholds) per owner and sends maintenance alert emails.
- **Sends email:** Yes, subject to the `system_email_enabled` company switch and per-event config.

### check-insurance-expiration

- **Schedule:** `0 */6 * * *` (every 6 hours)
- **Purpose:** Finds drone/D-Flight insurance policies nearing or past expiry per owner and sends expiry reminder emails.
- **Sends email:** Yes.

### reset-email-counters

- **Schedule:** `0 0 * * *` (daily, midnight)
- **Purpose:** Resets each owner's `daily_email_count` to 0 and updates `daily_email_count_reset_date` once per day, and clears `email_limit_notification_sent_date` so the limit-reached admin notification can fire again the next day. Skips owners already reset today (idempotent/safe to run more than once).
- **Sends email:** No — this is the daily-limit counter reset that other jobs depend on (see [Daily Email Limit Logic](/business-logic/email-notifications)).

### check-training-expiration

- **Schedule:** `0 6 * * *` (daily, 06:00)
- **Purpose:** Finds training/course certifications expiring within the configured reminder thresholds (30/14/7/3/1 days) per owner and sends training certification expiring emails.
- **Sends email:** Yes, subject to `training_email_enabled` and the `training_certification_expiring` event config. As of the `trainer_user_id`/course-owner change, the course owner (`training.trainer_user_id`) is also notified in addition to the configured roles/users.

## Adding a New Cron Job

1. Create the job and make sure it checks the `CRON_SECRET` before doing any other work.
2. Register its schedule in `vercel.json` under `crons`.
3. Add a row to the [Schedule Reference](#schedule-reference) table and a corresponding entry in [Job Details](#job-details) above.

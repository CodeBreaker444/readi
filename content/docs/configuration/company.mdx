The **Company** page lists every company on the platform. It is for platform administrators. Each company has its own users, systems and missions, and cannot see another company's data.

---

## Adding a company

Click **Add Company**. The form is split into sections:

| Section | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Basic Details** | Company code and name, legal name, company type (for example Private or NGO), email, phone, website |
| **Address** | Street address |
| **EASA Operator Code** | The operator registration code from the European Union Aviation Safety Agency, if the company has one |
| **Timezone** | The timezone used for the company's dates and times. Pick the nearest one, such as Central Europe, Italy / Spain, India, Japan, Singapore, Sydney or UTC |
| **Company Logo** | Upload a logo. You can crop it before saving. It is used on branded documents and reports |
| **Primary Admin User** | The first administrator: username, full name, email and phone. An activation email is sent to them after the company is created |

Phone numbers and similar details must be unique. If one is already used, the form tells you.

---

## Company details

Open a company to see its page, with tabs:

### Company Information
The core details above. Click edit, change them and save.

### Account Status
- **Company Active** switches the company on or off. **Disabling blocks all users in this company from logging in.** Switch it back to Active to restore access.

### Feature Flags
Switch platform features on or off for this company:

| Feature | What it gives |
|---|---|
| **Drone ATC** | The live airspace map ([Drone ATC](/docs/modules/drone-atc)) |
| **D-Flight** | D-Flight authorization and fleet ([D-Flight](/docs/business-logic/dflight-integration)) |
| **FlytRelay** | Flight logs and telemetry from drones |
| **Default Email Notifications** | Account activation, password reset and other default emails |
| **Operations / System / Training emails** | Separate switches for the emails of each area |
| **Daily Email Limit** | The most emails the company can send per day. The default is 100 |

Turn **Default Email Notifications** off only if you do not want activation and password reset emails to be sent.

### Admin account
The page shows the **Primary Admin User** of the company. If the admin forgets their password, use **Reset Admin Password**, enter and confirm a new one, and share it with them securely. If no admin is assigned, the page says so.

The **Knowledge Base** is where administrators upload company documents, such as procedures and manuals, for the Readi AI assistant to use. Once uploaded, the assistant can find and quote them when users ask questions.

Open it from the search menu at the top of the screen. Only **administrators** can use this page. Other users see "Access Denied".

---

## Uploading a document

1. Drag a file onto **Ingest New Document**, or click to choose one.
2. Wait for **Processing…** to finish. You will see "Ingested successfully".

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Accepted files | PDF or text |
| Size per PDF | 10 MB |
| Number of files | 10 in total |

When the limit of 10 files is reached the page shows **Storage Full**. Delete a file to upload another.

---

## Managing documents

**Active Knowledge Store** lists every uploaded document with its number of sections. Search the list by name.

- **Update a document:** upload the file again. Its content is refreshed.
- **Remove a document:** click the delete icon and confirm. Its sections are permanently removed from the assistant's knowledge.

<Warning>
The assistant can use anything you upload, so only add documents you are happy for your users to ask about.
</Warning>

The **Organisation** section of the sidebar describes how your operation is organised and the procedures people follow.

| Page | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Chart** | Show who reports to whom |
| **Procedures** | Define the steps of an operation |
| **Checklist** | Build the checklists pilots complete |
| **Assignments** | Define who or what is assigned to tasks |
| **Communication** | Define communication protocols |

Each one is explained, step by step, in [Procedures, Assignments and Communication](/docs/configuration/procedures-assignments).

## Main capabilities

- Create and manage user accounts (name, email, role, timezone)
- Role assignment: PIC, OPM, SM, AM, CMM, MM, TM, DC, SLA, ADMIN
- Viewer and Manager flags for additional permission layers
- Departments and qualifications for each person
- Territorial unit assignment
- Resend activation invites to pending users

## User activation flow

New users receive an activation email with a one-time link. Until activated, they appear as **Pending**. Use "Resend Invite" if the email was missed.

## Departments

Departments group your people, for example "Flight Operations" or "Maintenance". The department is shown beside a pilot's name when you assign them to a mission.

- **Assign a department:** choose it in the **Department** field when you create or edit a user.
- **Create or manage departments:** next to that field, click **Manage** to add a new department or remove one, without leaving the form.
- **Filter:** on the Team page, use **All Departments** to see one department.

---

## Qualifications

Qualifications are a person's licences and certificates, such as a pilot licence, with a start and an expiry date. They are shown when you pick a pilot for a mission.

### When creating a user

You can add qualifications before the user exists. In the **Qualifications** section, fill in the details and add them to the list. They are **staged**, which means they are only saved when you click **Save** and the user is created. You can stage up to 10 at a time. If saving the qualifications fails, the user is still created and you can add them later from the edit page.

### For an existing user

Open the user and expand **Qualifications** to see what is on file, add more, or remove one.

## Staff Profile

Everyone can open their own **Profile** from the menu at the top right.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Full name, phone, timezone | Your contact details. You can edit them. Timezone sets how dates and times are shown to you |
| **Email** | **Read-only** for most people. It is your sign-in address and shown greyed out. Only an Admin or Super Admin can change an email address. Ask your administrator if yours is wrong |
| **Department** | Pick from the departments your company has set up |
| **Signature** | Your signature text, used on documents that need one |
| EASA operator code | If your role requires it |

Click **Update Profile** to save.

Your **Training Curriculum** is shown in the profile too, split into **Active** and **History**, with each course, its certificate, completion and expiry dates and whether it is **Valid** or **Expired**. You can see your own curriculum at any time without asking an administrator. See [Training](/docs/modules/training) to add records.

## Clients

Open **Clients** in the Team menu to manage the companies you fly for. It shows the totals for **Total Clients**, **Active**, **Inactive** and **Expiring Soon** (contracts about to end).

- **Add New Client:** enter the contact details, location, website, payment terms and contract end date.
- **Search** by name, email, code or city, and filter by status.
- Each row has **Edit Client**, **Update Password** (the new password is emailed to the client) and **Delete Client**.

Clients you add here can then be chosen on missions and in the [Client Portal](/docs/modules/client-portal).

## Roles & Permissions

The **Roles & Permissions** page shows what each role can do by default. Each row is a feature and each column a role.

- Click a cell to switch between **Read-only** and **Full access**.
- Click **Save** under a role to store its changes.
- **OPM** and **Admin** always have full access and cannot be changed.
- A user without a personal override follows this table.

See [Role Permissions](/docs/business-logic/role-permissions) for what each role means.

Welcome to the official documentation for **Readi**, an operations and compliance platform for drone teams.

## What is Readi?

Readi helps aviation and drone operations teams manage planning, missions, training, compliance, maintenance, and records in one workspace. It is built for day-to-day operations while keeping your team audit-ready and giving everyone visibility of safety.

This documentation is written for the people who use Readi: pilots, operations managers, maintenance staff, safety and compliance managers and administrators. You do not need technical knowledge. The **Business Logic** and **Platform Rules** sections go deeper for people who want to know exactly how a rule works.

## What you can do in Readi

- Request evaluations and plan missions, with approvals and flight requests
- Run operations on a daily board and calendar, with weather help and D-Flight authorization
- Record every flight, battery and incident in the logbooks
- Manage drones and their components, and keep them airworthy with maintenance tickets
- Keep documents with versions, and link them to the equipment they apply to
- Run compliance with evidence tracking, audit plans and safety targets
- Track safety indicators and see your Safety Health Index
- Organise personnel, departments, qualifications, procedures and checklists
- Manage training courses, certificates and expiry dates
- Get alerts in one inbox, and ask questions to the AI assistant

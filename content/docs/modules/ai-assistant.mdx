title: "AI Assistant"
description: "Ask questions about your missions, fleet, alerts, compliance and maintenance in plain language."
---

The **ReADI AI Agent** answers questions about your own data, so you do not have to look through several pages. Open it with the **Ask anything** box at the top of the screen.

---

## What you can ask

Type a question in normal words and press **Enter** to send. For example:

- "How many missions have I completed?"
- "List active alerts"
- "Show open maintenance tickets"
- "What are my KPI values?"

It can answer about missions, fleet, alerts, compliance, maintenance and more. Click one of the suggested questions to start.

---

## What it can see

The assistant only shows what your role is allowed to see. A pilot only sees their own missions, for example, and a user never sees another company's data.

---

## Acting as an OPM (administrators)

An administrator can look at the answers an OPM would get:

1. Choose **Act as OPM user** and pick the person.
2. The banner shows "Acting as (name) (OPM)" and the placeholder says "Ask as (name)…".
3. Click **Clear OPM view** to return to your own view.

---

## Usage limit

The assistant has a usage allowance. When it is nearly used up, you see a warning such as "Only 10% remaining".

<Note>
The assistant is a helper, not a record. For anything official, such as an audit, check the page where the data lives.
</Note>

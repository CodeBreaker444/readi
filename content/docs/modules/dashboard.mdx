The Dashboard is the first page after login. It has two views, **Dashboard** and **SHI Index & KPIs** (Safety Health Index), both in the sidebar.

---

## Overview

### Totals

Cards at the top show the totals for the selected period (**This Year**, **This Month** or **This Week**):

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Missions | Missions in the period |
| Logged Drones | Drones that flew |
| Hours Flown | Total flight time |
| Km Flown | Total distance |
| Customers Served | Clients with at least one mission |

### Mission Results

A chart splits missions into **Completed**, **In Progress** and **Failed**, so you can spot problems early.

### Past and Next Missions

Two lists show what was flown recently and what is coming up. Each row has the mission ID, pilot, date and status. Use **Search** and **Filter** to narrow the list. Missions without a pilot show "Not Assigned".

<Warning>
Missions waiting for an OPM's approval, and companies using D-Flight where the authorization has not yet been accepted, do not appear as ready to fly. See [Mission Approval (OPM)](/docs/business-logic/mission-approval).
</Warning>

---

## Safety Health Index (SHI)

The **SHI Index & KPIs** page turns your safety indicators into a single score, based on the EASA model.

| Score | Status |
|---|---|
| 85% and above | **Excellent** |
| 70% to 84% | **Marginal** |
| Below 70% | **Unsatisfactory** |

The page shows:

- **Overall score** and its status
- **Safety score by area** (Compliance, Training, Operations, Maintenance), each with its number of indicators
- **Monthly trend:** current, average and peak score, and change from the previous month
- **Indicator cards:** each indicator's value against its target, marked Good, Marginal or Alert
- **Indicator trend:** select an indicator to see its history and how close it is to target

<Warning>
An indicator only counts once measurement values have been logged for it. See [Safety Management](/docs/modules/safety-management).
</Warning>

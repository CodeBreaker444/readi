---
title: "Drone ATC"
description: "A live map of your drones and the airspace around them."
---

**Drone ATC** (Air Traffic Control) shows your fleet on a live map, together with nearby aircraft, airspace zones and weather.

It is available from the **Control Center** menu to users with Drone ATC access (Admin, OPM and several other management roles).

---

## Before you start

Drone ATC needs your Control Center account to be connected (see [Control Center](/docs/modules/flytbase)). Without it you see "No Control Center API key configured", with a link to set it up.

The connection indicator at the top shows **Live**, **Connecting**, **Error** or **No API Key**. Use **Retry** if the connection fails.

---

## Fleet panel

The list on the side shows your drones with their state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Live** | Flying and sending its position |
| **Standby** | Available but not flying |
| **Offline** | Not connected |
| **No GPS** | Connected but no position yet |

Select a drone to follow it, and open its live feed or video if available.

---

## Map layers

Turn layers on or off from the **Layers** panel:

- **Traffic:** your drones, and other aircraft nearby
- **Airspace:** zones by class (A, B, C, D), with their height limits
- **Weather:** wind (animated), temperature, clouds, rain and snow, and air pressure

---

## Updating drones

Use **Sync Drones** (or **Update Drones**) to refresh the list of drones shown on the map from your Readi fleet. Do this after adding or changing a drone.

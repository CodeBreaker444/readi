---

**Emergency Contact List** (in the sidebar) holds your organization's emergency response plans: who to call and what to do in each type of emergency. Every plan belongs to your organization and is not visible to others.

---

## Plan types

| Type | Use for |
|---|---|
| General | Any emergency not covered below |
| Medical | Injuries and health emergencies |
| Fire | Fire and explosion |
| Security | Theft, intrusion, threats |
| Environmental | Spills and environmental incidents |

The page shows the total number of plans and how many are General or Specialized.

---

## Creating a plan

1. Click **Create ERP**.
2. Choose the **Type**.
3. Enter the **Contact** (for example a phone number).
4. Write the **Description**: the response steps and who to escalate to.
5. Click **Create Plan**.

Existing plans can be edited or deleted from the table. Deleting is permanent.

---

## Location groups

Contacts can also be organized by area. A **Location group** has a name, optional notes, an Active or Inactive status, a set of locations picked on the map, and the contacts responsible for them. You can select existing contacts or create new ones while saving the group.

---

## Who can use it

Most management roles can view and manage emergency plans, including Admin, OPM, Safety Manager, Accountable Manager and Responsible Manager.

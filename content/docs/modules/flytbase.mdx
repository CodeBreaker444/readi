The Control Center connection lets you bring flights flown with your drones into Readi, so they become part of your flight records.

---

## Connecting your account

Open **Control Center** in the sidebar and enter:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Name | Optional label, such as "Production" |
| API Token | A long-lived key from your Control Center account (not a short-lived login token) |
| Organization ID | Found in your Control Center dashboard under Organization Settings |

1. Click **Verify token**. Readi shows the account the token belongs to (name, username, phone, organization).
2. Check it is your account, then click **Yes, save this token**.

The token is stored securely and is never shown to other users. To change it use **Replace token**, or **Remove** to disconnect. After removal, flights from Control Center are no longer available until a new token is added.

<Warning>
If you see "No Control Center API token configured", ask your administrator to connect the account.
</Warning>

---

## Recent Flights

**Control Center → Recent Flights** lists the latest flights from your account. Choose a time window (last 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours, or the latest 20 flights) and select a flight to preview it.

The preview has three tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Map** | The route flown |
| **Flight Log** | Every recorded point: time, position, altitude, speed, heading and battery |
| **Flight Info** | Pilot, mission, start and end time, duration, distance, the drone, battery and controller details, and flight events |

---

## Saving a flight into Readi

Click **Archive to ReADI** to save the flight. Flights already saved show a **Saved to ReADI** label.

<Warning>
Add the drone and controller serial numbers to your equipment list first. If they are not registered, Readi asks you to register them before archiving.
</Warning>

An archived flight can then be sent to a connected DCC as the mission's flight report (see [DCC Integration](/docs/business-logic/dcc-integration)).

## C2 Config

**C2 Config** is for Administrators, OPMs and Super Admins. It controls which Control Center accounts your company can use and who may use them. It has two tabs.

### Organizations

Each entry is a Control Center account (for example "Production Environment").

- **Add Organization:** enter a name, the Control Center Organization ID and an API token. Readi checks the token before saving.
- **Delete:** removes the account from Readi, so anyone who used it loses access to its flights.

### Permissions

Lists your users and the organizations each one can use.

- **Edit User:** move organizations between *Available* and *Assigned*, then **Save Changes**.
- A user with no assigned organization cannot see flights from Control Center.

Deleting an organization and revoking a user's access ask for your [Authorization PIN](/docs/platform-rules/authorization-code).

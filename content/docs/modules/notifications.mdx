The **Notifications** page is your inbox inside Readi. Alerts about missions, maintenance and more arrive here.

At the top you see a summary such as "12 total - 3 unread messages".

---

## Categories

Tabs under the title split your notifications by topic:

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **All** | Everything |
| **Maintenance** | Maintenance alerts and ticket updates |
| **Mission** | Mission approvals, status changes and D-Flight messages |
| **General** | General messages |
| **Assignment** | When you are assigned to a mission |
| **Other** | Anything else |

A tab only appears when it has notifications. Each tab shows how many are unread.

---

## Filtering

Use the filters to narrow the list:

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Show **All**, **Unread** or **Read** notifications |
| **Procedure** | Show notifications from one area, for example `planning` |
| **From / To** | Only notifications between two dates |
| **Search message** | Look for a word in the message |
| **Reset** | Clear all filters |

---

## Reading and clearing

- **Mark as read** marks a single notification as read.
- **Mark all read** marks every unread notification as read in one click.
- **Delete** removes a notification you no longer need, after you confirm.
- **Reload** fetches the latest notifications.
- You can export the list from the export button.

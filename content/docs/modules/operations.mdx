Operations is where missions move from planned to completed. It has four views of the same missions, in the sidebar under **Operations**.

| View | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Mission Table** | Full list of missions with filters and details |
| **Daily Board** | Drag-and-drop board: Scheduled, In Progress, Done |
| **Calendar** | Schedule by week, month or list, and add missions |
| **Flight Requests** | Requests waiting for a decision (internal and external) |

Admins, OPMs and Super Admins also see **Email Notifications** to choose which mission emails are sent.

---

## Creating a mission

Click **Add Mission** (Calendar) or **New** (Mission Table). The **New Mission** screen has these parts:

1. **Client:** who the mission is for.
2. **Operation type and drone system:**

| Operation type | What it needs |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Pilot flies with the remote control. No mission plan |
| **PDRA** | Flies under a risk analysis. A mission plan is required |
| **STS-01 / STS-02** | Standard scenarios. Same as Open, no mission plan |

3. **Mission details:** category, type, location, date and time, pilot, procedure and notes.
4. **Recurring missions:** choose the days of the week and an end date to create the whole series at once.

### Systems you cannot pick

The list marks systems that are unavailable, with a reason:

- **Maintenance** or **Maintenance Due:** under maintenance, or due soon (review before scheduling)
- **Not Operational:** a component has expired. Creation is blocked
- **Dismissed:** administratively retired
- **SN Mismatch:** no system for that client has the serial number

### Companies using D-Flight

For Open, STS-01, STS-02 and PDRA missions you also draw the flight area on the map (a circle, or the plan's route for PDRA). See [D-Flight Mission Authorization](/docs/business-logic/dflight-authorization).

### Missions created by pilots

A pilot's mission goes to the OPMs of their department for approval before it appears on the Daily Board. See [Mission Approval (OPM)](/docs/business-logic/mission-approval).

## Daily Board

Each mission is a card in one of three columns: **Scheduled**, **In Progress** or **Done**. Drag a card to the next column to start or finish the mission.

Cards show the pilot, the system, and its maintenance state (**Maintenance OK**, **Alert**, **Due** or **In Maintenance**). Open a card for the timeline (planned, official start and end, actual start and end), people and equipment, and flight results.

Before a card can move, Readi checks that:

- The system is Operational and not in maintenance
- The pilot has filed today's declaration (below)
- The mission has OPM approval, if it needed it
- D-Flight has accepted it, if your company uses D-Flight
- You have set an **Authorization PIN**, which you enter to confirm the move

See [Mission Status Gates](/docs/platform-rules/mission-status-gates) and [Authorization Code (PIN)](/docs/platform-rules/authorization-code).

### Daily Pilot Declaration

Each day a pilot must confirm, before starting a mission, that they:

- are rested and not fatigued
- are physically and mentally fit
- are not under medication that could affect performance
- have reviewed the mission plan and area
- have inspected the equipment and drone systems

All items must be ticked before submitting.

### Procedure tasks

If the mission has a company procedure, its tasks appear on the card. Complete them before finishing the mission. When done the card shows "All tasks completed".

## Completing a mission

When a mission is finished, **Mission Complete** has three tabs:

| Tab | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Maintenance** | Record the flights, hours and days used on each component. Usage can be automatic or manual. It can only be recorded once per mission |
| **Flight Logs** | Attach the flight log, by uploading a file (.zip, .json, .xml) or fetching it from Control Center |
| **Post-Flight Data** | Result, notes, distance and other final details |

A component that reaches its maintenance limit shows "Maintenance is overdue". See the [Maintenance Module](/docs/business-logic/maintenance-module).

## Importing a flown mission

Use **Import mission** to add a flight that was already flown. It takes four steps: **Client**, **Log File** (upload a .gutma or .zip file, or pick a flight from Control Center), **Mission Data** and **Pilot**.

## Calendar

Shows missions as colored entries: Scheduled, In Progress and Completed. Switch between **Today**, **Week**, **Month** and **List**.

### Best Time to Fly

The **Best Time to Fly** tab next to the calendar shows the next 7 days as a grid of flight windows (06:00 to 22:00), colored for the drones you fly through Drone ATC.

- **Location:** click the location field to open the map. Search for a place, click the map to drop a pin, then choose **Use this location**. The crosshair button uses your current position. The choice is remembered.
- **Windows:** each cell is one window (length set by **Slot length**, 1 to 4 hours) with the average wind, the peak gust, the total rain, a weather icon and a score out of 100. A cross means a limit is exceeded; `--` means night.
- **Colors:** green is good to fly, amber is marginal (close to a limit), red is no-go, grey is night. Hover a cell to see what is limiting it.
- **Best slot:** the best upcoming window is outlined in violet with a star.
- **Show:** switch the large number in each cell between the overall score, wind, gusts and rain.
- **Drones:** pick one or more of your drones (active drone components with a serial number), or leave **All fleet types**. With many drones, use the **+N** list to search. With several selected, a window is only green if every one of them can fly it. A drone with no model set uses generic limits.
- **Limits:** limits belong to the drone model, so drones of the same model share them. They start from a manufacturer default; use the gear icon on a drone to set your own.

The forecast is advisory only. Always check official aviation weather before you fly.

## Flight Requests

Has two tabs:

- **Internal:** missions created by your pilots that need OPM approval. Approve, or deny with an optional reason. Filter by All, Pending, Approved or Denied.
- **External:** requests from outside systems and clients (see [DCC Integration](/docs/business-logic/dcc-integration) and [Client Portal](/docs/modules/client-portal)). Requests are counted as New, Acknowledged or Assigned. An OPM can **Move to Planning** (assign it to a planning mission and a plan), or deny it.

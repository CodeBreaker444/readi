Safety Management in the sidebar contains **SPI & KPI Definitions**. The results are shown on the [Dashboard](/docs/modules/dashboard) as the Safety Health Index. **Emergency Contact List** also lives here: see [Emergency Response Plans](/docs/modules/emergency-contact).

---

## SPI and KPI

| Type | Stands for | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **SPI** | Safety Performance Indicator | Measuring safety, such as number of incidents |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator | Measuring performance, such as on-time maintenance |

Each indicator belongs to an **area**: Compliance, Training, Operations or Maintenance.

The page shows the total indicators, active monitors and the Operations area count. Filter by area and by Active or Inactive, and search by name.

---

## Creating an indicator

Click **New Indicator** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Code | Capital letters, numbers and underscores, at least 3 characters. **Cannot be changed after saving** |
| Name, Description | What it measures |
| Type and Area | SPI or KPI, and its area |
| Unit | For example %, hours or count |
| Target Value | The goal. Must be positive |
| Target Direction | **Higher is Better** or **Lower is Better**. For incidents choose Lower |
| Reporting Frequency | Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly |
| Status | **Active / Monitoring** or **Disabled / Draft** |

---

## Logging a measurement

An indicator does not appear on the dashboard until you log a value.

1. Click **Log measurement value** on its row.
2. Enter the **Measurement Date** and the **Actual Value**.
3. The status is worked out for you:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green | On track, or better than target |
| Yellow | At risk, or exactly on target |
| Red | Breached, or worse than target |

Which side of the target is good depends on the Target Direction.

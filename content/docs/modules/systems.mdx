A **system** is a drone and everything it is built from. Each part (the drone itself, a battery, a motor, a controller) is a **component** with its own serial number and usage. Open **Systems** in the sidebar to manage them. This page covers the tools beyond adding and editing records.

---

## Systems map

**Systems → Map** (Drone System Map) shows your installed docks and drone systems on a map.

- **Filters:** by **Status** (Operational, Not Operational, Maintenance, Decommissioned), by **Client**, and a search box (for example a system code). **Docks only** and **Installed only** narrow it further.
- **Legend:** the symbols are the Control Center, Dock, Drone and Cluster (several systems close together), coloured by status.
- **Tool List:** a table of the systems on the map. **Locate** jumps to one on the map, and **Detail** opens its details: general info, specifications and its components.
- **Panel tabs:** **Systems** shows the components attached to a system, and **Warehouse** shows the spare components that are not on any system, with their serial numbers.
- A system with no location is not shown on the map ("No map location available for this item").
- **Reload** refreshes the map.

---

## Location name

A system can have a **Location Name** next to its map coordinates, for example "Warehouse A". It makes lists, exports and the Maintenance Logbook easier to read than raw coordinates. Missions have one too (see [Operations](/docs/modules/operations)).

## QTB report (PDF)

The QTB report is a printable flight record for a drone. It is available on **drone** components.

1. In the components list, find the drone and choose **QTB Report**.
2. Pick a **start date** and an **end date**. They use your own timezone, and both start as today.
3. Click **Generate PDF**. The file downloads straight away.

The end date must be on or after the start date. If you choose a very long period you may see "too many flights"; just narrow the dates and try again.

## Duplicate System

Use **Duplicate System** to set up a new drone that is like an existing one, without typing everything again.

1. Choose **Duplicate** on the system you want to copy.
2. **System Details** tab: the details are copied and the code ends in `-copy`. Change the **Code** and choose the **Client** (both are required). You can also change the description, location, status and activation date.
3. **Components** tab: every component of the original is copied as a new one, and you can review them one by one. Codes and serial numbers also end in `-copy`, so replace them with the real ones. You can edit the type, name, model, dates, vendor, maintenance cycle and parent component of each.
4. Click **Save Duplicate**.

The copy is a brand-new system with its own components. The original is not changed.

## Component relations

**Relations** lets you organise the parts of a system into a simple family tree, for example a battery and a camera that belong to a drone.

- Open **Relations** on a system. Every component has a **Parent** dropdown.
- Choose a parent to make a component a **child** of another. Choose **None (top-level)** to take it out again.
- **Live Preview** shows the result before you save. Click **Save** to apply it.

There is only **one level**:

- A component cannot be its own parent.
- Only top-level components can be parents. A child cannot have children of its own.
- A component that already has children cannot become a child. Remove its children first.

### Primary component

In the same window, choose the **Primary Component** of the system, normally the drone itself. It is marked **PRIMARY** and is the component shown for the system in mission details. Only one component can be primary at a time.

## Warehouse: moving and reassigning components

Spare parts that are not on a drone live in the **Warehouse**.

- To take a component off a system, **detach** it and confirm. It moves to the warehouse. A component that is still attached to a system cannot be deleted, so detach it first.
- The **Warehouse** tab, in the components list and on the systems map, shows what is available, with serial numbers.
- To use a warehouse component again, assign it to a system. This is how you swap a battery or move a part from one drone to another.

Components in the warehouse are not offered as systems for missions. Detaching is recorded as a secure transaction in the [Audit Logs](/docs/records/audit-logs).

## Component Log and Flights

Each component has two views of its own history:

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Log** | A timeline for this component, such as location changes and the missions it was used on |
| **Flights** | The flight logs for this component |

Use them to answer "how much has this battery been used?" or "where has this part been?".

---

## Models, component types and drone classes

Drop-down lists such as the model, type and class of a component are managed by you, from the system screens.

- **Models:** use **Add Model** to describe a drone or other equipment once and reuse it. A model holds the brand, model code and name, type and sub-type (for example multirotor, fixed wing, VTOL or dock), weight, operating conditions (wind and temperature range), performance (endurance, speed, altitude), the maintenance cycle and the guarantee. Use **Edit Model** to change one later. The wind and temperature limits are the ones used for the weather forecast in [Operations](/docs/modules/operations).
- **Manage Component Types:** add, rename or remove the types you can choose for a component.
- **Manage Drone Classes:** add or rename the drone classes.

---

## Insurance and alerts

A component can store its **insurance** details: the insurance name, company and **expiry date**. To be warned before it expires:

- **Notify Users:** choose which users are emailed.
- **Days before:** how many days before expiry the reminder is sent.
- **Additional Emails:** add people who do not have a Readi account.

---

Training has three pages in the sidebar: **Courses**, **Calendar** and, for Admins, OPMs and Super Admins, **Email Notifications**.

---

## Courses

Training records for each person and their certificates.

The page shows the total number of records. Filter by **Valid** or **Expired**, or by user, then **Clear** to reset.

### Adding a record

Click **New** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Users | Choose one or several people. The same record is created for each |
| Course Name and Type | What was taught |
| Session | The session it belongs to |
| Certificate Type | **Participation** (attended) or **Qualification** (passed an exam) |
| Session date, Completion date | When it took place and ended |
| Expiry date | When the certificate stops being valid |

Records can be edited or deleted. Deleting removes that person's record for the course.

### Training curriculum

Open a person to see their **Training Curriculum**, split into **Active** and **History** (older, expired records). Each row shows the course, type, certificate, session, completion date, expiry date, and a **Valid** or **Expired** status.

### Recompute KPI

**Recompute KPI** recalculates the monthly Training indicator from attendance records, which feeds the [Dashboard](/docs/modules/dashboard).

---

## Calendar

Shows scheduled training sessions. Open a session to see each participant, the session date and their status: Completed, In Progress, Absent or Other. Use **Refresh** to reload.

---

## Email Notifications

Admins, OPMs and Super Admins choose which training emails are sent: when a record is created, updated or deleted, and a **Certification Expiring** reminder sent 30, 14, 7, 3 and 1 days before expiry. Nothing is sent if email notifications are disabled for the company.

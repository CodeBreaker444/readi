---
title: "Getting Started"
description: "A step-by-step first-day and first-week guide to setting up Readi."
---

This guide takes you from a new account to your first completed mission. Do the steps in order, because each one builds on the one before.

---

## Day 1: get in and secure your account

1. **Activate your account** with the link in the invitation email, then sign in.
2. **Set up two-factor authentication** with an authenticator app when Readi offers it after your first password change.
3. **Set your Authorization PIN** under **Settings → Security**. You need it to start and finish missions.
4. Open your **Profile** and check your name, timezone and department.

See [Settings and Security](/docs/configuration/settings-security).

---

## Day 1: set up your company (administrators)

1. **Company:** check the company details, logo and timezone ([Company](/docs/configuration/company)).
2. **Team:** invite your people, give each a role and a department, and add pilot qualifications ([Team Management](/docs/configuration/team)).
3. **Clients:** add the clients you fly for.
4. **Mission Definitions:** adjust the mission types, categories, statuses and results to your needs ([Mission Definitions](/docs/configuration/mission-definitions)).
5. **Organisation:** add procedures, checklists, assignments and communication protocols ([Procedures, Assignments and Communication](/docs/configuration/procedures-assignments)).

---

## Day 2: add your equipment

1. Add each **system** (drone) and its **components**, with serial numbers ([Systems](/docs/modules/systems)).
2. Organise the parts with **Relations** and choose the **primary component**.
3. If you use D-Flight or Control Center, connect them so drones and flights come in automatically ([D-Flight](/docs/business-logic/dflight-integration), [Control Center](/docs/modules/flytbase)).
4. Upload manuals and certificates to the [Document Repository](/docs/records/document-repository).

---

## Week 1: plan and fly your first mission

1. Send a **New Evaluation Request** and follow it to a positive result ([Planning](/docs/modules/planning)).
2. Create a mission in **Operations**, choose the drone, pilot and observers ([Operations](/docs/modules/operations)).
3. On the day, the pilot files the **daily declaration**, and the mission is moved across the **Daily Board** with the PIN.
4. When it is done, complete the mission: record component usage, attach the flight log and add the post-flight data.
5. Check the flight in the **Flight Logbook** ([Logbooks](/docs/modules/logbooks)).

---

## Week 1: stay safe and compliant

- Add a **training** record for each pilot and check the expiry dates ([Training](/docs/modules/training)).
- Add one **compliance requirement** and attach evidence ([Compliance](/docs/modules/compliance)).
- Define a **safety indicator** and log its first value ([Safety Management](/docs/modules/safety-management)).
- Check **Maintenance** and open a test ticket to see how it works ([Maintenance](/docs/modules/maintenance)).

---

## Good habits

- Read your **Notifications** every day ([Notifications](/docs/modules/notifications)).
- Ask the [AI Assistant](/docs/modules/ai-assistant) when you cannot find something.
- Review the [Audit Logs](/docs/records/audit-logs) now and then.
- See [Release Notes](/docs/modules/releases) to learn what is new.

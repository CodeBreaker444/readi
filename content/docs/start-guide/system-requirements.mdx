---
title: "System Requirements"
description: "What you need on your computer or phone to use Readi without problems."
---

Readi runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to install. You need a recent browser and a working internet connection.

---

## Browser

Use an up-to-date browser:

| Browser | Minimum version |
|---|---|
| Chrome | 110 or newer |
| Firefox | 110 or newer |
| Edge | 110 or newer |
| Safari | 16 or newer |

If something looks wrong or a button does nothing, update your browser first. It fixes most problems.

---

## Screen

Readi works on a computer, tablet or phone. Tables and maps are easiest to use on a larger screen. On a phone, the Daily Board and Calendar are shown one day at a time.

---

## Internet

- A stable connection is needed. Maps, the weather forecast and live flight data are loaded as you use them.
- If your company uses a firewall or proxy, ask your IT team to allow Readi and its email service. Otherwise pages, maps or emails may not load.

---

## Permissions your browser may ask for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Only if you use the crosshair button on a map to use your current position. You can also search for a place or tap the map instead |
| **Pop-ups** | Allow them for Readi, because some PDFs and documents open in a new tab |
| **Downloads** | Needed for exports and reports, such as the QTB report, logbook exports and document downloads |

---

## Files you upload

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Documents in the Document Repository | 25 MB, PDF, DOCX, XLSX, JPG, PNG or TXT |
| Event attachments | 100 MB each, JPG, PNG, MP4 or PDF |
| Knowledge Base files | 10 MB per PDF |

---

## Signing in

- You need an **authenticator app** on your phone (such as Google Authenticator or Authy) to set up two-factor authentication. See [Settings and Security](/docs/configuration/settings-security).
- You need an **email address** that you can read, to receive your activation link.

---

## Roles and access

Features are limited by role, so make sure each person has the right role before you give them access. See the [Role Glossary](/docs/business-logic/role-glossary).

## Features

### Component Activation Date in Maintenance

Each component card in the maintenance screens now shows its activation date alongside its last maintenance update, so you can see how long a component has been in service at a glance. This is visible both in the maintenance tab of the Mission Complete modal and in the Update Maintenance modal on the operations daily board.

## Bug Fixes

### Cron Jobs Not Triggering

Fixed an issue where scheduled cron jobs were being rejected by the authentication middleware and failing to run. Cron requests authenticated with the server's secret token are now correctly recognized and allowed through.

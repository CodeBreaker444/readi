## Features

### Company Logo Upload

Companies can now upload a logo — from the company create page or the company edit screen's General Info tab. The logo appears next to the company name in the company list, on the company detail page, and in the top bar next to your organization name.

### Company Logo on QTB Reports

The uploaded company logo now renders in the top-left of every generated QTB (Technical Logbook) report, right before the report heading, matching the official template layout.

## Improvements

### Map Tiles Fixed

Leaflet maps across the app (system/component location picker, planning, drone ATC, flight requests, emergency contact locations) were showing a "API KEY REQUIRED" watermark because the previous tile provider now requires a paid API key. All maps have been switched to a free, key-free tile provider, restoring normal map rendering with proper labels in both light and dark themes.

## Bug Fixes

### DCC Drone ID Lookup for Reassigned Systems

Fixed a bug where looking up a mission plan's DCC drone ID only checked the first tool ever assigned to that plan. Plans where the assigned system/tool changed over time could return the wrong (or no) drone ID. The lookup now checks all tools that have been assigned to the plan.

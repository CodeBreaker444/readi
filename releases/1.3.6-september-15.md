## Features

### Drone ATC Coverage Expanded to Spain

Drone ATC now covers Spain in addition to Italy. Airspace zones and live flight tracking are both pulled for the expanded region, and the map's default view, bounds, and zoom now center on the combined Italy/Spain coverage area.

### Animated Wind Flow Layer

The wind layer on the Drone ATC map has been upgraded to a live, animated particle flow (powered by a gridded wind-vector field) that visualizes wind speed and direction across the current map view, refreshing automatically as you pan and zoom.

### Crop Logos Before Upload

Company logos can now be cropped, rotated, zoomed, and repositioned in a preview dialog before they're uploaded, so the saved logo always fits cleanly wherever it appears — the top bar, company list, company detail page, and QTB reports.

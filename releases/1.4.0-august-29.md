## Features

### Mission Group Filter in Flight Logbook and Mission Table

You can now filter both the flight logbook and the mission table by mission group, using a dropdown populated with the group labels already in use. This makes it easy to pull up every mission that belongs to a specific recurring or grouped series.

### Battery Serial Number in Flight Logbook Reports

Flight logbook exports (CSV, Excel, and PDF) and the on-screen table now include the battery serial number used on each mission, sourced from the maintenance records logged when the mission was completed.

### Notes Included in Flight Logbook Reports

Mission notes — whether entered when the mission was created or added afterward during post-flight completion — are now included in flight logbook exports alongside the existing on-screen column.

### Recurring Mission Indicator

A rotating-arrows icon now appears next to the mission group badge wherever it's shown — the mission table, its detail view, and the flight logbook — but only when that mission is part of a recurring series. One-off missions with a group label show the label without the icon.

## Bug Fixes

### Pilot Qualifications Showing Expired Credentials

When assigning a pilot to a new mission, the qualifications panel previously listed every qualification on file, including expired or inactive ones. It now only shows qualifications that are currently active.

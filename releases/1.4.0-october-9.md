## Fixes

### Maintenance Counters Reset When a Ticket Is Closed

Closing a ticket now reliably resets the maintenance hours and flights of the components it covers. Previously, only the last maintenance date was updated and the counters stayed the same.

### Correct Mission Status on the Dashboard

A mission marked as Completed in Operations no longer appears as In Flight on the Dashboard.

### Emergency Contact Page Opens Directly

The Emergency Contact page now opens correctly when you go to its address directly, not only through the sidebar.

### Detached Components No Longer Appear in Maintenance

A component moved out of a system (for example, to the Warehouse) no longer shows up in the Basic or Standard maintenance selections of its old system.

### Drone Models Reject Invalid Values

Drone models can no longer be saved with negative weight, speed, altitude, or flight time.

### Training Records Check Dates

A training record can no longer be saved when the completion date is before the session date, or when the expiry date is before the completion date. A clear message explains what to correct.

### Clearer Message When Adding a System

Submitting the Add System form with missing information now shows a clear message about which fields are required, instead of an error.

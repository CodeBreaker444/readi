## New

### Customise Your Reports

Clicking Export and choosing CSV, Excel or PDF now opens a window where you pick what goes into the report. Every column has a checkbox, with a Select all option, and only the ticked columns are downloaded. This works on the maintenance, flight logbook, mission, ticket, user and audit log tables, and everywhere else the Export button is used.

### Your Company Logo in Reports

The same window has an Include company logo option. PDF reports show your company logo in the top-right corner of every page, and Excel reports show it in a banner above the table. If your company has no logo yet, the ReADI logo is used in the PDF. CSV files are plain text and cannot contain a logo, so the option is switched off for CSV.

## Fixes

### Readable Dates in CSV

The last and next maintenance dates in CSV exports opened in Excel as ### when the column was narrow. They now show as normal dates.

## New

### Two Client Statuses

Each client now has two independent statuses. **Status** is controlled by an admin and can be used to block a client for security reasons. **Email** shows whether the client has activated their account through the link emailed to them, and appears as "Verified" or "Not activated" on the Client Management page. A client can log in only when both are active.

### Admin Activation

If a client has not activated their account by email, admins (Admin and Super Admin roles) see an **Activate Account** action in that client's row menu. It activates the account right away, and the emailed link stops working. The action disappears once the client is activated, and it does not change the admin-controlled Status. The change is recorded in the activity log.

### Inactive and Not Activated Clients in Dropdowns

Client dropdowns and filters now also list clients whose Status is Inactive. They are labeled "Inactive", greyed out and cannot be selected. This applies to the New Operation, Import Operation, Evaluation, System, Personnel and Event settings forms, and to the Operations, Logbook, Mission Template and Systems Map filters. Clients that have not activated their account are labeled "Not activated". That label is informational only: these clients can still be selected.

## New

### Pilot Groups and Audience

The OPM can organise pilots into custom **Pilot Groups** (for example Experts, Newbies, External) and choose which groups a slot group is **Offered to**. Only members of those groups see and request the slots. With no group selected, every pilot sees them. The group picker has a search box and a scrolling list, and hovering a group shows its description and members with picture, name, role and email.

### Scheduled Publishing

A slot group can be published now or scheduled for a later date and time. Pilots do not see it until then, and are notified within a few minutes of it opening.

### Assign a Pilot Directly

From any slot, the OPM, Admin or Super Admin can assign a pilot. The list ranks pilots by availability and score, shows overlapping assignments, and puts expert group members first for tough-weather slots. Other pending requests for the slot are declined automatically. The assigned pilot, and pilots who were declined, get an in-app notification and an email. Unassigning notifies the pilot with a warning.

### Day Preview

A new **Day Preview** shows every slot of one day with its client and assigned pilot. Unassigned slots are shown in red so gaps are easy to spot.

### Slot Weather

Slots in the next 7 days show a calm, moderate or tough weather indicator, with wind, gusts and rain for the slot's hours. The weather is taken at the location of the system chosen for each slot group, so every client's group can have its own forecast. Groups created without a system (or with a system that has no location) show no weather, and a warning says so while you create the group.

### Pilot Merits

**Pilot Merits** scores every pilot from 0 to 100 over the last 90 days: 40% weekend availability, 30% overall availability and 30% hours flown. Pilots see their own hours, score and rank on the Slot Booking page.

### Slot Calendar

A new **Calendar** tab shows seven days of every slot in one view: slot group, client, assigned pilot, waiting requests, flying hours and weather. Click a slot or a day for the full details, and assign a pilot from there. Search by group, client or pilot, filter by client or slot group, and filter unassigned slots. Slots of different slot groups with the same time window show side by side, and a pilot on two overlapping slots (for example for two clients) is flagged as a collision, red when assigned to both and amber when one is only a request.

### Monthly Flight Limits

Set a monthly flight limit for each pilot in the **Flight limits** tab of a pilot group, and choose the day the flying-hours cycle starts in Slot Settings. When approving, assigning and on the calendar, the OPM sees the pilot's hours as flown/limit: yellow from 90%, red from 100% and grey when no limit is set.

### Pilot Details When Approving

When reviewing a request, the OPM now sees the pilot's total flights, flights in the last 7 days (weekend and total), merit score and rank, pilot groups, how many slots of this slot group the pilot asked for, and whether they asked for weekend slots.

### Move a Request

Pilots can move a pending request to another slot of the same group. A configurable lock window (never, 24 or 48 hours before the approval day) prevents last-minute changes.

## Improvements

### Same Times for Different Clients

The duplicate slot warning (merge, overwrite or cancel) now only compares slot groups of the **same client**. Slot groups for different clients can use the same dates and time windows, such as 06:00–14:00 twice on one day, and Merge and Overwrite never touch another client's slots.

### Client Required

Choosing a client when opening a slot group is now required. The client list has a search box and scrolls when long.

### Cleaner Create Form and Settings

Start and end hours are picked from a compact hour grid instead of a long dropdown, and the lock setting uses simple buttons.

### My Requests Tab

For pilots, **My requests** is now its own tab next to the available slot groups, with the request count shown in its label.

### Assigned, Not Requested

A slot the OPM assigned directly now shows as **Assigned** instead of Requested, in the slot dialog and in the pilot's request table. The Assign pilot panel is hidden once a pilot's own request has been approved.

### Cached Weather

Slot weather is now stored in the database and refreshed in the background, so the calendar, Day Preview and assign list load faster. The screens show when the forecast was last fetched.

### Documentation

The Slot Booking documentation, database tables and cron jobs pages were updated for all of the above.

## What's New

### Flight Requests Are Checked Against Operation Zones

Every flight request from a client, sent from the Client Portal or from the client's DCC, is now checked against the **operation zones** of all the systems assigned to that client.

- **Inside the zones of a system:** the request arrives as usual. Readi notes the **nearest** system that covers the location and shows it in the new **Zone check** column. That system is used when you create a mission for the request.
- **Outside the zones of every system:** the request is **rejected automatically**. The reason names the nearest system and its distance. The client's DCC receives a denial with that reason, and a Client Portal user sees it straight away and can correct the location.
- **Not checked:** requests without coordinates, or for a client with no system that has operation zones, arrive as before.

A location is inside a system's zones when it is inside the perimeter, inside a green zone (if the system has any) and clear of red zones, red lines and unsurveyed zones.

### Link to Mission Replaces Move to Planning

After acknowledging a request, you now **link it to a scheduled mission** instead of moving it to a planning:

- **Create a new mission.** Opens the Mission Table with the New Mission form ready. A header shows the request details (request ID, client, location, nearest system, requested time and time received). The form is pre-filled with the client, the nearest system, the start time and the location. When you save, the mission is linked to the request.
- **Link an existing mission.** Choose one of the client's planned or in-progress missions.

The client's DCC is told the request was accepted, with the drone of the mission's system.

### Request Status Follows the Mission

You no longer set a flight request to In Progress, Completed or Issue by hand. Once linked, the request follows its mission on the **Daily Board**: when the mission starts, the request is **In Progress**. When the mission is completed, it is **Completed**. When the mission is aborted or cancelled, it is **Issue**. The client's DCC is updated at each step. A new **Mission** column shows the linked mission and its status. If the mission is deleted, the request goes back to **Acknowledged** so it can be linked again.

### Pilots Fly Their Own Flight-Request Missions

When a pilot (PIC) creates a mission from a flight request, the pilot list shows only that pilot, already selected. This includes pilots who are also managers. Choosing another pilot is refused by the server too. OPMs and admins can still choose any pilot, and visual observers can still be any pilot.

### No OPM Authorization for Zone-Validated Requests

A pilot's mission for a flight request **does not need OPM approval** when the request passed the zone check and the **selected system** covers the requested location. A mission already waiting for approval is approved automatically when it is linked to such a request. If the system does not cover the location, or the mission is created normally with New Mission on the Mission Table, the usual approval applies.

### Scheduled Flights in the Client Portal

Clients now have a **Scheduled Flights** page in their sidebar. It shows the approved flight slots of the slot groups created for that client, one day at a time, starting with today. For each flight the client sees:

- The time window, in the site's time zone
- Whether it is **Upcoming**, **In progress** or **Finished**. This updates on its own while the page is open
- The pilot flying it: picture, name, email and phone
- The system and the weather forecast, when the slot group has a system
- The client's own missions linked to the slot, with their progress

Clients can move to the previous or next day or pick a date. Only approved slots are shown. Slot group names, open slots, pending requests and other clients' slots are never shown. A slot group with a future publish date stays hidden until it is published, the same as for pilots.

## Fixes

### Missions Could Start Without OPM Approval

A pilot's mission is supposed to wait for an OPM's approval before it can be started. A pilot could get around this and run a mission that no OPM had approved. This is now closed in every place we found:

- **Creating a mission as already started.** A mission created by a pilot now always begins as **Planned**, whatever the form or request asked for. It stays that way until an OPM approves it.
- **Editing a mission.** While a mission is waiting for approval (or was denied), it cannot be marked in progress or completed by any means, including changing its status or setting a start or end time. Cancelling or aborting it, setting it back to **Planned**, and ordinary edits such as notes still work.
- **Attaching a flight log to a new mission.** Missions created this way by a pilot now also go through OPM approval, and the approvers are notified. A pilot cannot create them as already started.

## Improvements

### Pilots Who Are Managers Need Approval Too

Previously a pilot who was also a manager could create and run missions without approval. Now **every pilot** needs an OPM's approval for the missions they create. Until it is approved, the mission does not appear on the Daily Board and cannot be moved there. OPMs and admins are not affected, and they remain the only ones who can approve or deny a request.

If your organisation has pilot-managers who relied on running missions straight away, an OPM now needs to approve those missions first.

## Notes for Admins

**Database update required.** Run `prisma/manual-sql/051_flight_request_zone_validation.sql` before deploying. It adds the zone-check columns to flight requests.

Requests received before this update were not zone-checked, and keep their current status. Requests already moved to a planning keep working for flight logs. New links are made to missions. The manual status endpoint `PATCH /api/planning/flight-requests/{id}` has been removed.

Client Portal requests now record the client they came from. Before, they showed no client on the Flight Requests page.

Missions that were started before this update without approval are not changed. To find them, look for missions that are in progress or completed but still show **Pending** or no approval status, and that were created by a pilot.

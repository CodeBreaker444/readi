## Fixes

### Missions Could Start Without OPM Approval

A pilot's mission is supposed to wait for an OPM's approval before it can be started. A pilot could get around this and run a mission that no OPM had approved. This is now closed in every place we found:

- **Creating a mission as already started.** A mission created by a pilot now always begins as **Planned**, whatever the form or request asked for. It stays that way until an OPM approves it.
- **Editing a mission.** While a mission is waiting for approval (or was denied), it cannot be marked in progress or completed by any means, including changing its status or setting a start or end time. Cancelling or aborting it, setting it back to **Planned**, and ordinary edits such as notes still work.
- **Attaching a flight log to a new mission.** Missions created this way by a pilot now also go through OPM approval, and the approvers are notified. A pilot cannot create them as already started.

## Improvements

### Pilots Who Are Managers Need Approval Too

Previously a pilot who was also a manager could create and run missions without approval. Now **every pilot** needs an OPM's approval for the missions they create. Until it is approved, the mission does not appear on the Daily Board and cannot be moved there. OPMs and admins are not affected, and they remain the only ones who can approve or deny a request.

If your organisation has pilot-managers who relied on running missions straight away, an OPM now needs to approve those missions first.

## Notes for Admins

Missions that were started before this update without approval are not changed. To find them, look for missions that are in progress or completed but still show **Pending** or no approval status, and that were created by a pilot.
